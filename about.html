Artisan Spotlight · Alwar Studio

The Alchemy of Terracotta & Fire

Master potter Ramkishan and his family have shaped red grog clay on the outskirts of Alwar for four decades. Each pour-over carafe and serving vessel is thrown on a stone momentum wheel before being slow-baked in wood-ash kilns.

"Clay has memory," he explains. "If your mind is rushed, the rim wobbles. If your breath is steady, the vessel stays true for generations."
